Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains, in simple words, what information JobOps collects and how we use it.

1. What we collect

  • Account info: your name and email address.
  • Profile info: your resume, skills, and anything else you add.
  • Usage info: which pages you visit and which jobs you view, to make JobOps better.

2. How we use it

  • To show your profile to employers when you apply for a job.
  • To send you emails about your applications (like "application received").
  • To keep JobOps working well and to fix problems.

3. What we never do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not give your contact details to advertisers.

4. Cookies

We use small files called cookies to keep you logged in and remember your settings. You can clear them in your browser at any time.

5. Your choices

  • See or edit your info any time in your profile settings.
  • Ask us to delete your account and all your data: we will remove it.

6. Security

We protect your data carefully, but no system is perfect. Please use a strong, unique password and keep it safe.

Questions? Contact us at privacy@jobops.example.
